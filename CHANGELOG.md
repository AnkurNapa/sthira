# Changelog

All notable changes to Sthira. Versions follow docs/RELEASE.md.

## 0.3.4 (7 November 2026)

### Changed
- The warning triangle is gone from the welcome page and every caution note. Cautions keep their warm colour instead.

## 0.3.3 (7 November 2026)

### Safety
- Saved plans are rebuilt from your answers on every visit, so every safety fix reaches you straight away.
- Breathing practices your plan avoids can no longer be started.
- Knee plans leave out the kneeling lunge in sun salutations and Tree Pose. Seated twist steps and imported flows respect your plan.

### Added
- Key alignment cues for every pose, spoken during each hold and shown on screen and on pose pages.
- Calm background music: singing bowls by default, Om chanting, ambient, flute, rain and ocean.
- Real photos for 15 more poses. Poses without a photo show a calm name card instead of a drawing.

### Fixed
- 25 issues from a full test pass, including double taps, Back during a session, saving when you leave mid session, small screens and offline photos.
- The page name shows in the top bar instead of a second Sthira.

## 0.3.2 (7 November 2026)

### Safety
- A full content audit against a trainer reference: 29 fixes. Dizziness and low blood pressure now leave out inversions, arm balances and fast breathing. Stronger cautions for Skull Shining Breath, Wood Chopping, Locust, forward folds and more. Downward Dog is left out for shoulder injuries.

### Changed
- Counter poses added to Morning Wake-Up, Stamina and Flexibility.
- The 15 Minute Meditation ends with a seated closing.
- Feature tiles line up their pictures across each row.

## 0.3.1 (7 November 2026)

### Added
- A share card for links on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, X and iMessage.

### Changed
- Final touches to the APM screens. The offline cache moves to version 4.

## 0.3.0 (7 November 2026)

### Added
- Real photos for 32 poses and 9 sun salutation positions, from Pexels, with photographer credits.
- The APM method: every practice runs asana, then pranayama, then meditation. Five guided meditations in the Emma voice.
- APM balance in the flow generator: Asana focus, Balanced APM or Breath and mind.
- 19 poses, 11 breathing practices, 11 articles and 4 sessions: Moon Salutation Flow, Spine Care, 15 Minute Meditation and Sun Salutations A and B.

### Changed
- Sessions that rely on poses your plan avoids are no longer offered.
- Opening one detail page from another replaces it. Back returns to the previous one.

## 0.2.0 (7 November 2026)

### Added
- A welcome page that explains Sthira, with a feature tour and the safety note.
- A Progress dashboard: rings, streaks, period totals, a minutes chart, a 12 week calendar, journey progress, badges, history, export and import.
- Opening and closing rituals in guided sessions and generated flows, read in the Emma voice.
- Two articles: Begin and end your practice. Opening and closing a class.
- About the teacher page and credits for Ankur Napa, RYT 500.
- Photos and looping video from Pexels and Mixkit, with a credits page.
- Apple style navigation: a sticky product bar, desktop flyouts and a full screen mobile menu.

### Changed
- Spoken cues now play in the Emma voice instead of the browser voice.
- Flows of 10 minutes or less skip the sun salutation so they fit the time.

## 0.1.0 (7 November 2026)

First public version.

### Added
- 70 poses with steps, mistakes to avoid, an easier version, benefits and who should skip them.
- Pose illustrations for 58 poses and every sun salutation position.
- 7 guided sessions with a timer, chime and the Emma voice.
- A 10 question quiz with a dosha profile, health filter and 6 month journey.
- A flow generator and a builder for your own flows.
- 7 breathing practices with a pacer.
- Progress rings, streaks, badges and history kept on your device.
- 9 learning articles on safety, set up and practice.
- Offline use after the first visit.

### Safety
- Two independent reviews of every pose and condition list.
