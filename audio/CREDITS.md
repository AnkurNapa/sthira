# Audio credits

Background audio for Sthira. The singing bowls were made in code for this site. The Om chant is a public domain (CC0) recording from the Internet Archive. The other tracks come from Mixkit under the Mixkit free licence, which allows use in websites and apps without attribution. Credit is given here anyway.

Each track is a seamless loop of about 90 seconds. Recorded tracks use a calm section of the original, quietened to about -24 LUFS so it sits under a spoken voice. The originals are not changed in any other way. The Om chant also has a gentle low cut to remove rumble.
